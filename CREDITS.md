## Uyari

**Alexander Salazar** — <https://www.linkedin.com/in/malexandersalazar/>

Uyari was conceived, designed and built by Alexander Salazar, who leads the project. He is the author of:

* the problem statement ([`PROBLEM.md`](PROBLEM.md)) and the methodology ([`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md));
* the five use cases with AI models: Indigenous text ↔ official-language text (both directions), Indigenous
  speech ↔ official-language speech (both directions), and speech-to-text for the Indigenous language;
* the data model and the whole technical infrastructure for any under-served language: the data format,
  validator and builds, corpus generation, the training and evaluation pipelines, the agents and the demo.

## The Shawi–Spanish case

Shawi ↔ Spanish is the first language pair. It began with maternal health: Shawi communities meet health
services that offer no support in their language. This case is co-led by:

* **Alexander Salazar** — the data model.
* **Yuri Cavero** — <https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuri-cavero-29248a29/> — coordination with the translators and
  contact with the Shawi community.

The Shawi–Spanish translations in this repository were financed and organized by Alexander Salazar and Yuri
Cavero, who hold the rights to them.
