## The Shawi–Spanish translation corpus

Shawi ↔ Spanish is the first language pair. It began with maternal health: Shawi communities meet health
services that offer no support in their language.

The Shawi–Spanish translations in this repository (contributions `2026-11_uyari_0001` to `0003`) were financed
and organized by Alexander Salazar and Yuri Cavero, who hold the rights to them and release them under
CC BY 4.0 ([`DATA_LICENSE.md`](DATA_LICENSE.md)).
