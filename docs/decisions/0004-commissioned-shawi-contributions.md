# 0004 The Shawi translations: three commissioned contributions

The Shawi translations were commissioned and paid for by Alexander Salazar and Yuri Cavero, the leads of the
Shawi–Spanish case, who hold the rights to them.
They arrived in three batches and are filed as three contributions (`2026-11_uyari_000{1,2,3}`) under the
`commissioned-work.md` terms; row ids are pseudonymous. License and collection dates stayed `TODO`, with
`validate --strict` failing, until the rights holders set them on 2026-11-02: **CC BY 4.0** (commercial use,
including by companies deploying for clinics, with credit; see `DATA_LICENSE.md`), collected 2026-06-01 to
2026-11-01. Translation continues; later work becomes new contributions, since merged ones never change.

Four rows whose Spanish did not match their sentence (shifted cells, an unreadable cell, a different
sentence) were held out rather than guessed; no row was silently dropped.
