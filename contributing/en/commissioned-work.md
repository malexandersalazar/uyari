# Commissioned work

Used by contributions whose translations or recordings were commissioned and paid for by a person or an
organization that holds the rights to them and chooses their license. The individuals who produced
commissioned work are not identified in the repository; row ids are pseudonymous.

* Purpose, acceptable use and withdrawal are the same as in [`consent-collective.md`](consent-collective.md).
* The license is set per contribution in `contribution.yaml` by the rights holders.
* `compensated: yes` is recorded; amounts are not.
* Recordings of a person's voice always also need that person's individual consent
  ([`consent-individual.md`](consent-individual.md)), whoever paid for them.

The first Shawi contributions (`2026-11_uyari_0001` to `0003`) are commissioned work; their rights are held by
Alexander Salazar and Yuri Cavero, who lead the Shawi–Spanish case.
